Feedbacker deep dive · 01

A feedback loop that starts the conversation.

We built the operational pipeline that could contact a customer after a visit, carry the exchange, and turn the result into action.

QUEUEDSENTIN PROGRESSCOMPLETE / ESCALATE

The problem 01

Most feedback tools wait for the customer.

Feedbacker reversed the direction: initiate a timely conversation, preserve its state, and surface the cases that require a human.

The operating flow 02

  1. 01TriggerA visit creates a queued session.
  2. 02ReachWhatsApp starts the conversation.
  3. 03ListenWebhooks capture replies and status.
  4. 04DecideSentiment updates the next step.
  5. 05CloseComplete, reward, or escalate.

What was implemented 03

Conversation is only one layer.

Orchestration. Per-customer sessions, message history, conversation state and final review creation.

Reliability. Queued jobs, signed webhooks, encrypted provider credentials, message deduplication and delivery tracking.

Operations. Response, completion and escalation KPIs with a traceable session view.

The architecture supports an external conversation service and includes deterministic fallback behavior.

Where this capability can travel 04

The pattern is reusable.

Potential applications include post-appointment follow-up, onboarding check-ins, incident intake, renewal-risk conversations, and service audits.
