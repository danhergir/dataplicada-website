Feedbacker deep dive · 02

Make customer comments readable at scale.

We built a multilingual pipeline that transformed raw feedback into sentiment, themes, searchable evidence, and assisted analysis.

BUENOSERVICELENTOEXCELLENTATENCIÓN

The problem 01

The signal was buried in free text.

Service teams needed to compare comments across customers, dates and languages without reading every response one by one.

The data flow 02

  1. 01IngestForms, conversations and CSV files.
  2. 02NormalizeClean text and translate to English.
  3. 03ScoreCombine two sentiment methods.
  4. 04OrganizeKeywords, dates and company context.
  5. 05ExploreSearch, trends and assisted analysis.

What was implemented 03

Models stayed connected to evidence.

Language pipeline. Automatic translation plus a VADER and TextBlob sentiment ensemble normalized to a five-point scale.

Pattern layer. Keyword cleaning, frequency by date, company-specific endpoints and comment search.

Interpretation. Review classification, cited phrases, explanations and an analyst conversation with stored memory.

FastAPI · Python · Pandas · SQLAlchemy · VADER · TextBlob · language models

Where this capability can travel 04

Free text exists everywhere.

Potential applications include multilingual survey analysis, employee comments, review monitoring, service-quality audits, and document-based issue classification.
