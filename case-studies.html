01 / AYDEA CONSULTORES

Management consulting
Data engineering

THE QUESTION

How can customer-service and agile project work become measurable inside the tools the team already uses?

Context

Operational information was distributed across workflows, making it difficult to connect customer-service activity with project execution and management visibility.

Our work

Dataplicada designed a data architecture and measurement plan around Monday.com, defining the information flows and indicators needed for clearer operational oversight.

Value created

A practical foundation for tracking service performance, refining internal processes, and making agile work easier to understand and improve.

02 / EMERGE

Sales training & human development
Research + machine learning

THE QUESTION

Which brand name best supports the market position—and what evidence can make that choice defensible?

Context

A consequential naming decision needed more than subjective preference. The team required a structured view of audience response and the language surrounding each option.

Our work

We combined market research, survey design, sentiment analysis, and text-network visualization to compare alternatives from multiple evidence sources.

Value created

A clearer, evidence-backed basis for selecting the name most aligned with the intended audience and strategic direction.

03 / FEEDBACKER

Customer experience
AI + NLP product

THE QUESTION

How can smaller service teams learn from customer feedback without enterprise-scale analytics resources?

Context

Customer comments contain important patterns, but manual review makes those patterns slow and difficult to use consistently.

Our work

Dataplicada built a customer-intelligence product using natural-language analysis, RAG agents, and AI-assisted workflows.

Value created

A more accessible way for service teams to organize feedback, surface themes, and turn qualitative input into action.
