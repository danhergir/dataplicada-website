Case study · Emerge

Choose a name with evidence.

We turned audience language around eight candidate names into a clear branding decision.

SURVEY8 OPTIONSSENTIMENTTEXT NETWORKSDECISION

The challenge 01

A strategic name could not be chosen by instinct alone.

A new human-development, leadership, and sales-training brand needed to understand which candidate name best resonated with its pharmacy-sector audience.

The analysis 02

  1. 01AskDesign a structured survey around eight candidate names.
  2. 02ClassifyCompare positive, neutral, and negative opinions.
  3. 03MapVisualize relationships between words and perceptions.
  4. 04DecideBring the evidence together around one option.

What we delivered 03

More than a popularity vote.

Audience research. A structured survey captured preferences and the language behind them.

Opinion analysis. Sentiment and subjectivity made the tone of feedback comparable across all eight options.

Text networks. Visual maps exposed the associations, patterns, strengths, and weaknesses surrounding each name.

Survey design · Machine learning · Text visualization

The result 04

One name, backed by audience evidence.

The combined analysis gave the founder a clearer view of how each option was perceived and supported the selection of the strongest brand name.
