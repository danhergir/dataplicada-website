Case study · Service operations

Turn customer follow-up into a managed workflow.

We designed a system that reaches customers at the right moment, understands their response, and brings a person in when needed.

SERVICE MOMENTCONVERSATIONDECISIONHUMAN HANDOFF

The problem 01

Good feedback often arrives too late.

The goal was to follow up while the experience was still fresh, without asking a team to manage every exchange manually.

The operating flow 02

  1. 01StartFollow up after a service moment.
  2. 02ListenKeep the conversation and its context together.
  3. 03DecideUse the response to choose the next step.
  4. 04Hand offSend sensitive cases to the right person.

What was implemented 03

The value was in the handoff.

Timely outreach. Start a conversation after a visit instead of waiting for a survey response.

Safe operation. Preserve the conversation, avoid duplicate messages, and track delivery.

Team visibility. Show which conversations were answered, completed, or escalated.

Delivered as a secure, multi-company messaging workflow.

Where this capability can travel 04

Useful wherever follow-up matters.

The same pattern can support post-appointment check-ins, onboarding, incident intake, renewal conversations, and service audits.
