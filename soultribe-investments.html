Case study · SoulTribe Investments

Turn investment reporting into a repeatable system.

We created a system to manage investor projections, record capital changes, and generate updated branded reports.

SIMULATETRACKUPDATEREPORT

The challenge 01

Every change meant rebuilding the numbers.

Investment projections required analyst-led calculations, and each capital movement or reinvestment change meant updating the model before a new investor report could be produced.

The solution 02

  1. 01ConfigureDefine the investment terms and create the investor record.
  2. 02SimulateCalculate growth, dividends, reinvestment, and future scenarios.
  3. 03RecordRegister capital movements and changes over time.
  4. 04ReportGenerate an updated branded report from the latest information.

What we built 03

One investment record from projection to reporting.

Investment simulation. Calculate projected capital, dividends, and reinvestment based on each investor's terms.

Change history. Record capital entries, withdrawals, and reinvestment changes over time.

Branded reporting. Generate downloadable reports showing current position, historical activity, projections, and upcoming disbursements.

Financial simulation · Capital tracking · Investor reporting

The result 04

Update the record. Update the report.

SoulTribe gained a consistent way to manage investment changes over time and generate updated, branded reports from the latest investor information.
