Selected work · Product engineering + applied AI

We built a feedback system from collection to action.

Feedbacker was a Dataplicada product for service teams. We designed and engineered the web application, analysis API, data workflows, and customer follow-up tools behind it.

VOICE OF CUSTOMER · MULTI-TENANT SOFTWARE

Case brief 01

THE CHALLENGE

Help smaller service teams collect feedback and find useful patterns without reviewing every comment manually.

OUR ROLE

Product strategy, UX, full-stack engineering, data pipelines, NLP, AI integration, and iteration.

THE SYSTEM

A multi-tenant web app, a separate analysis API, MySQL data, and messaging and reward workflows.

THE RECORD

The repositories show active development from 2022 through 2026. The product is no longer offered.

The operating problem 02

Feedback only matters when a team can use it.

The hard part was not collecting another rating. It was connecting collection, language analysis, and follow-up in one workflow.

01Collect

Reach customers close to the service moment.

02Understand

Turn comments into searchable, comparable signals.

03Act

Give teams a clear next step or escalation.

What we built 03

A full feedback stack—not a single model.

The system evolved in modules. Each layer handled a distinct part of the business process.

01 / INPUTS

Collection channels

  • Branded feedback forms
  • Company QR codes
  • CSV imports
  • WhatsApp conversations
02 / APPLICATION

Multi-tenant operations

  • Company and user accounts
  • Authentication and subscriptions
  • Rewards and reference IDs
  • PQRS case workflows
03 / INTELLIGENCE

Analysis service

  • Translation and sentiment ensemble
  • Keyword-frequency processing
  • AI classification and explanations
  • Conversational analysis with memory
04 / OUTPUTS

Decision support

  • Ratings and comment dashboards
  • Keyword trends and search
  • Suggested actions
  • Escalations and follow-up

The working interface 04

Overview and evidence stayed together.

Teams could move from aggregate indicators back to the comments that produced them.

Capabilities delivered 05

01

Multi-channel collection

Forms, QR, bulk uploads, and later proactive WhatsApp sessions.

02

Company-specific analytics

Comments, ratings, keywords, trends, and filters separated by organization.

03

Multilingual NLP

Translation plus VADER and TextBlob sentiment scoring for imported comments.

04

AI-assisted interpretation

Classification, consequences, cited phrases, summaries, and an analyst chat with memory.

05

Service recovery

Negative-sentiment escalation, PQRS tracking, and traceable reference IDs.

06

Participation workflows

Configurable rewards, eligibility, claims, and customer follow-up.

IMPLEMENTATION

Two applications.
One operating system.

PRODUCT APPLICATION

Laravel · PHP · MySQL · queues · webhooks · subscriptions · Respond.io

ANALYSIS API

FastAPI · Python · SQLAlchemy · Pandas · VADER · TextBlob · LangChain · OpenAI · Gemini

RELIABILITY CONTROLS

Bearer authentication · signed webhooks · company isolation · message deduplication · fallback conversation logic

What we accomplished 06

We turned an idea into an end-to-end software product.

01 Built the customer-facing collection experience, authenticated workspace, analytics API, and data layer.

02 Connected AI analysis to real product actions: search, trends, summaries, rewards, and escalation.

03 Evolved the system from forms and QR codes into queued, stateful WhatsApp feedback conversations.

04 Created reusable Dataplicada capability in document and language workflows, multi-tenant data, and applied AI.
